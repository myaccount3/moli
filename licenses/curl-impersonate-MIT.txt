curl-impersonate (MIT) — TLS/HTTP2 fingerprint profile subset
================================================================

Source: https://github.com/lexiforest/curl-impersonate
File: patches/curl.patch (targets curl 8.23.0)
Upstream license: MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 curl_cffi developers

Moli vendors a trimmed subset of that patch into the lexmount/curl-rust fork
(curl-sys/curl). Only the fingerprint-related CURLOPT_* options, the chrome152
profile table, the vtls/openssl.c BoringSSL-like configuration block and the
lib/http2.c / lib/http.c HTTP/2 knobs are retained. See
docs / spike reports (section2-triage.md, transform.py T0-T9) for the exact
trimming and AWS-LC compatibility transformations. The full license text
follows.
